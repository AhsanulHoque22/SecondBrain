# 01 — Master Plan (Exam Season: 29 May → 15 Jul 2026)

## The reframe
This is **not** a 10-day sprint. It's a **7-week, 6-exam marathon** with one exam every
Wednesday. That regular spacing is a gift: you build **one repeatable weekly engine** and
reuse it five times. Your only irregular window is the first one — AI gets 12 days because
nothing is studied yet. After that, every subject gets exactly 7.

---

## Windows 2–5 — the Weekly Engine (reuse for InfoSec, Compiler, Distributed, Graphics)

Each runs Wed→Wed:

```
Wed (exam afternoon)  Reset + load next subject: syllabus, past-paper pattern, rank topics by yield
Thu                   Core block 1 (highest-yield topic)
Fri                   Core block 2 + recall of block 1
Sat                   Core block 3 + first untimed past paper
Sun                   Repair weak areas + practice problems
Mon  ◄ last 2 days    SUBJECT ONLY: timed past paper + revision. Livora = 0.
Tue  ◄ last 2 days    SUBJECT ONLY: cheat sheet, final recall, sleep early.
Wed                   EXAM 10:30 AM → then load the next subject.
```

- **Window 2 — InfoSec (CSE 717):** 10 → 17 Jun
- **Window 3 — Compiler (CSE 711):** 17 → 24 Jun  *(historically heavy — phases, grammars, parsing. Give it your sharpest mornings.)*
- **Window 4 — Distributed & Cloud (CSE 719):** 24 Jun → 1 Jul
- **Window 5 — Computer Graphics (CSE 715):** 1 → 8 Jul  *(math-heavy: transformations, rasterization. Practice the numericals.)*

## Window 6 — Thesis (CSE 700) · 8 → 15 Jul
Different beast — likely a defense/viva or submission, not a memorization exam. During this
week your "study blocks" become "thesis blocks": polish document, build slides, rehearse
the viva (your public-speaking hour finally pays off directly). We'll plan this in detail
once the 5 written exams are behind you.

---

## Where everything else fits
- **Livora (2h/day max):** parked in one afternoon block, ending by Asr. Zero in every exam's last 2 days.
- **Public speaking (1h/day):** non-negotiable, post-Maghrib. Doubles as thesis-viva prep.
- **Competitions:** list them in `04_Livora/README.md` with dates. We slot prep into the 2h Livora budget; if a competition lands in an exam's last-2-days, we flag the conflict early and pre-load the prep.
- **Skill development:** during exam season, skill dev = the public-speaking hour + whatever Livora work teaches you. Don't add more now.

## The rule that makes this work
**Topics finished, not plans finished.** Every day ends with at least one topic moved from
"learning" to "can explain it cold." That's the only metric that passes exams.
