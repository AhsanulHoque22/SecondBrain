# Public Speaking — 1h/day

Your startup edge *and* your thesis-viva prep. Non-negotiable, usually post-Maghrib.

## Simple daily structure (pick one, 1h)
- 10 min: warm-up (tongue twisters, breathing)
- 20 min: prepared — record a 3–5 min talk (pitch Livora, or explain today's study topic out loud — doubles as active recall!)
- 20 min: watch back, note 2 fixes
- 10 min: impromptu — random topic, 2 min, no prep

## Tip
Explaining today's AI/Compiler topic out loud *is* the Feynman technique. One hour that
serves studying + speaking + viva prep at once.
