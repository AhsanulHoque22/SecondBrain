# 06 — Relationships

> Quality connections protect mental performance during exam season.
> Budget 30 min/day. This is not optional — it's maintenance.

---

## The Exam-Season Connection Strategy

### Why bother during exams?
Isolation amplifies stress. One quality conversation/day = lower cortisol, better sleep,
better recall next morning. 30 minutes invested here pays for itself in study hours.

### Quality over quantity
- 1 real conversation > 10 WhatsApp reply exchanges
- Intentional contact: you initiate, you listen, you're present
- Not multitasking — be actually there for 15 minutes

---

## Daily (30 min, post-Asr block)

**Pick one:**
- Call or voice message a family member (parents, siblings)
- Check in with a close friend — a real question, not "sup"
- Study accountability partner: 2-min daily sync (planned/done)

**What to say if you're short on time:**
> "Hey, I'm in exam prep mode but I wanted to check in. How are you actually doing?"

That one question, asked genuinely, is worth more than 10 "lol" exchanges.

---

## Weekly (60 min, fits in lighter Livora day)

- One deeper conversation: a friend you haven't properly talked to in 7+ days
- Or: collaborative study session (invite someone to study nearby)
- Or: virtual co-working session (silent zoom call — just being present)

---

## Study Accountability Partner

Find one person also doing exam season.

**Daily 2-min sync (Telegram/WhatsApp):**
```
Morning: "Today I'm doing: [topic]. You?"
Evening: "Done ✓ / Not done (reason). Tomorrow: [topic]."
```

This creates social stakes — you're less likely to slack when someone expects an update.
