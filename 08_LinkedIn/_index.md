# 08 — LinkedIn Posts

> Continuous folder for regular LinkedIn posting. Each topic becomes a **series** — linked posts that build on each other instead of one-off content. Claude Code plans the series, brainstorms angles, researches supporting material from the rest of this vault, drafts posts, and keeps this index current.

## How this works
- Trigger via the [[scripts/data/protocols/linkedin_posts|LinkedInPosts protocol]] — say `"LinkedInPosts"`, "write my next LinkedIn post", or "start a new series on X".
- Each series lives in `08_LinkedIn/Series_[Name]/` with its own `_series.md` tracker plus one file per post.
- Posts wikilink back to whatever vault content they draw on (course wiki pages, [[04_Livora/README|Livora]] log, daily logs) so research is never repeated between posts.
- The protocol itself has a running "evolution log" — it updates its own steps as patterns repeat, instead of staying static.
